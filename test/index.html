Talk Time with Dad

Five questions a topic, starting easy and ending with a story. Read the starter line out loud, then finish it in your own words.

Pick a topic

Tips for whoever is chatting with him
Quiet room, face to face

Turn off the TV. Sit where he can see your face and you can see his.

One question at a time

Wait at least 10 seconds before helping. Silence is thinking time.

Slow and big

Remind him to slow down, take a breath first, and say each word fully, whatever his therapist has taught.

Don't finish his sentences

If you missed a word, ask for just that word again, or ask him to spell or point.

One topic a day

Five questions takes about 10–15 minutes. Stop early if he is tired.

Real interest

Respond to what he says, not how he said it. The goal is a real chat.
